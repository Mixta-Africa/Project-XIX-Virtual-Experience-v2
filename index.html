Investor Narrative

The commercial
case

Polo-led destination estates outperform standard residential on scarcity, rental yield, and brand premium. Project XIX builds each of these levers in.

01
Destination Identity

A polo estate is a category of one in West Africa. The FIP-standard field, equestrian compound, and Clubhouse create a singular address that standard residential cannot replicate.

02
13 Revenue Streams

Real estate sales, polo memberships, tournament hosting, Clubhouse hospitality, commercial lets, waterfront premium, events, coaching, advertising, horse industry services, health & wellness, digital, and short-let.

03
Lakowe Corridor Position

Ibeju-Lekki's infrastructure investment — Lekki Free Zone, Dangote Refinery, new road corridors — positions Lakowe as the next premium address tier. Project XIX is placed at that frontier.

04
Mixed-Use Resilience

223 residential units across six typologies, commercial block, and hospitality infrastructure provide multiple absorption channels and reduce single-typology concentration risk.

05
Mixta Africa Track Record

Developer of Lakowe Lakes Golf and Country Estate — an established exit comparable in the same corridor — with multi-phase delivery capability and a DFI-grade development model.

06
Comparable Premiums

Lagos polo corridor estates command 92–213% premium over VI/Lekki comparables. Global benchmarks: Val-de-Vie (South Africa), Al-Habtoor Polo Resort (Dubai), Grand Polo Club — Emaar (Dubai).
