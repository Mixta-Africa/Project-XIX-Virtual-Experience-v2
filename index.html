Investor Narrative

The commercial
case

Polo-led destination estates outperform standard residential on scarcity, rental yield, and brand premium.

01
Destination Identity

A polo estate is a category of one in West Africa. The FIP-standard field, equestrian compound, and Clubhouse create a singular address that standard residential cannot replicate.

02
13 Revenue Streams

Real estate sales, polo memberships, tournament hosting, Clubhouse hospitality, commercial lets, waterfront premium, events, coaching, advertising, horse industry services, health & wellness, digital, and short-let.

03
Lakowe Corridor Position

Ibeju-Lekki's infrastructure investment — Lekki Free Zone, Dangote Refinery, new road corridors — positions Lakowe as the next premium address tier.

04
Mixed-Use Resilience

223 residential units across six typologies, commercial block, and hospitality infrastructure provide multiple absorption channels.

05
Mixta Africa Track Record

Developer of Lakowe Lakes Golf and Country Estate — an established exit comparable in the same corridor — with multi-phase delivery capability.

06
Comparable Premiums

Lagos polo corridor estates command 92–213% premium over VI/Lekki comparables. Global benchmarks: Val-de-Vie, Al-Habtoor Polo Resort, Grand Polo Club Emaar.
